AI-powered job board & career ecosystem

Job Dekho

↗

A cron-driven asynchronous ETL pipeline that decouples job publishing from LLM enrichment, using Gemini to extract structured skills, categories and experience levels from raw postings — plus an independent LLM-as-a-Judge layer (NVIDIA Nemotron / Llama 3.1 70B) that grades the pipeline against a hand-curated adversarial dataset.
